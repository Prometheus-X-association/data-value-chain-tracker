# Maintenance

DVCT is maintained by **Scheer IMC** until **30 June 2027**: a 12-month period after the original end of the Prometheus-X project (until 31 December 2026), followed by a 6-month extension.

Maintenance keeps the existing features installable, buildable and running with up-to-date, secure dependencies. It covers dependency updates, security fixes, small bug fixes and documentation corrections. It does not cover new features, architecture or smart-contract changes, or major framework upgrades.

The full maintenance policy, including scope, contacts and what happens after the period ends, is in [MAINTENANCE.md](https://github.com/Prometheus-X-association/data-value-chain-tracker/blob/main/MAINTENANCE.md).

## Reporting issues

- Bugs: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Prometheus-X-association/data-value-chain-tracker/issues)
- Security vulnerabilities: follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Prometheus-X-association/data-value-chain-tracker/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.
